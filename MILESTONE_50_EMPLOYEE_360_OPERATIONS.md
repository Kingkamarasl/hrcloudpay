# Milestone 50 — Employee 360° Production Operations

The Employee 360° experience is treated as an operational workspace, not merely a profile card.

## Existing operational foundation surfaced
- Employee records and ID card number.
- Department and manager relationships.
- Contracts and contract dates.
- Employee documents and compliance evidence.
- Warning letters and disciplinary history.
- Attendance and leave relationships.
- Salary and position change workflows.
- Employment events / activity timeline.
- HR request routing for manager-controlled changes.
- Audit logging for sensitive employee changes.

## Frontend addition
A dedicated Employee Operations hub links the Employee 360 workflow to directory, departments, compliance and request tracking.
