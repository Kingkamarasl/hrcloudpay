# Milestone 52 — Attendance & Leave Operations

HRCloudPay now presents attendance and leave as a connected workforce-operations domain.

## Existing capabilities
- Attendance records.
- Leave and break requests.
- Leave accrual policies and balances.
- Leave encashments.
- Request tracking.
- Manager/HR approval workflows.
- Employee-scoped permissions.
- Audit events for approvals and rejections.

## Frontend addition
The Workforce Operations hub connects attendance, leave, accruals and request tracking from one operational entry point.
